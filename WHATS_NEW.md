# What's new in Rocket Cast 1.8.2

- **Base Colors presets.** Save your team colors as named presets next to the color pickers. The selected preset is your base: it's applied when you pick it, remembered between launches, and **Reset Team Data** now goes back to it instead of the stock blue/orange.
- **Match Replay Mode.** A new overlay toggle for scrubbing saved match replays. It stops the goal pop-up from firing on every goal you scrub past and keeps the player card visible during replays.
- **Steadier bottom player card (RLCS).** The name/boost box is now a fixed width, and long names shrink to fit instead of stretching the card.
- **Goal slides stay put (RLCS).** Rocket League sends a second "goal scored" event when the replay shows the goal. That used to snap the slides closed and open again. They now stay out from the goal until the next kickoff.
- **Side flyouts stay open.** The TOGGLE / OVERLAY flyouts no longer close on their own while you're typing in them or have your mouse over them.

Camera feeds, highlight reels and the other account features are part of [Rocket Cast +](README.md#rocket-cast-).
